About Us

Big Tech, Bite-Sized

Tech CKS is a tech content page from Kolkata, India, built for one simple idea — you should not need an engineering degree to understand new technology. We break down Artificial Intelligence, Coding, Cybersecurity & Future Tech into short, clear Reels of 10 to 30 seconds, published in both Hindi and English — along with daily AI & tech news posters, so you never miss what matters. About the Creator

The Person Behind Tech CKS

CS

Chandan Singh

Full-Stack Software Developer

Kolkata, India

Chandan Singh is a full-stack software developer and technology enthusiast from Kolkata, India — and the creator behind Tech CKS. He started the page with a simple belief: complex technology should be easy for everyone to understand, no matter which language they speak.

Through Tech CKS, he creates short tech explainers in Hindi and English on artificial intelligence, coding and cybersecurity — distilling big, fast-moving ideas into clear Reels of just 10 to 30 seconds, so anyone can learn something new in less than a minute.

Beyond content, Chandan builds software of his own. He is the developer of VoiceFlowAI, an offline Android voice-to-text app — an example of his hands-on, builder-first approach to technology. His mission with Tech CKS is to make AI and tech knowledge simple, fast and accessible to everyone, in the language they are most comfortable with.
